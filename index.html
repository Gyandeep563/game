Hardware & Input Latency Disclosure

Your measured reaction time includes the human neural response plus your hardware stack: display refresh rate (a 60Hz screen adds ~16.7ms frame delay vs ~4.1ms on 240Hz), browser rendering pipelines, and input polling (wired mice poll at 1000Hz while standard Bluetooth devices can introduce 15–30ms latency). ReflexRush isolates software overhead for the cleanest possible measurement.

Understanding Reaction & Hardware Latency

Your total measured time is the sum of biological processing and hardware lag:

  • Display Refresh Delay: A standard 60Hz monitor presents a new image every 16.67ms. A 144Hz monitor drops this to 6.94ms, and a 240Hz screen to 4.16ms.
  • Input Device Polling: A gaming mouse polling at 1000Hz reports every 1ms. Standard Bluetooth wireless mice or phone touchscreens often exhibit 10–25ms of input lag.
  • Audio vs Visual Cues: Humans process auditory signals faster (140–160ms) than visual signals (180–220ms) because acoustic nerve pathways are shorter.

ReflexRush uses browser-level performance.now() with microsecond accuracy to eliminate code-level overhead.
